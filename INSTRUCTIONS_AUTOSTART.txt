HOW TO ENABLE AUTOSTART FOR AEGIS-GOLD
=====================================

To make your Trading Dashboard appear automatically when you open your laptop:

1. Right-click the file "run_aegis.bat" in this folder.
2. Select "Show more options" -> "Create shortcut" (or just "Create shortcut").
3. Press "Windows Key + R" on your keyboard.
4. Type "shell:startup" and press Enter.
5. Move the shortcut you created into the window that just opened.

That's it! Next time you open your laptop and log in, your MetaTrader 5, Bot Backend, and Dashboard will all start automatically.
